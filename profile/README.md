# 🌍 MeDiTwin — Mediterranean Digital Twin for Climate Extremes

**MeDiTwin** is an EU-funded Horizon Europe project (Grant Agreement No. 101159723) aiming to develop an **AI-powered Mediterranean Digital Twin** to **monitor, model, and mitigate the impacts of climate extremes** — such as droughts, floods, heatwaves, and wildfires.

- Coordinated by: National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)
- Partners: Max Planck Institute for Biogeochemistry, University of Valencia (UV), European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF)
- Duration: 2024–2027
- Funded under: Horizon Europe (HORIZON-CSA – Coordination and Support Actions)

## Vision

> *"A smarter, more resilient Mediterranean through trustworthy AI and digital twins."*

We bring together Earth Observation, AI, and climate science to provide **actionable intelligence** for environmental resilience, supporting **EU DestinE**, **Green Deal**, and **Digital Europe** initiatives.
